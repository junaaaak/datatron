What Datatron woke up to

Datatron opened its eyes and blinked. The map on the wall showed nothing. His Spotify had no songs to suggest. Every screen was blank.

Without data, even simple tools stop working.

No app could guess what Datatron wanted to watch, buy, or hear next. Every recommendation, every prediction, every "you might also like" was gone.

Predictions come from patterns in old data.

So Datatron made a decision: it would learn exactly how data works, one piece at a time, and get the world running again. That's where you come in.

Four labs. One mission. Let's start.
